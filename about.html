About OptionPayoffCalc

OptionPayoffCalc is a free set of options calculators built by an independent options trader. It started as a personal tool to check covered calls and cash-secured puts before placing trades, and grew into a small collection of calculators for common strategies.

What you can do here

  • Options profit calculator for any combination of calls, puts and stock
  • Covered call calculator
  • Cash-secured put calculator
  • Wheel strategy calculator
  • Iron condor calculator

How the calculations work

Theoretical option prices and Greeks use the Black-Scholes model with a continuous dividend yield. Probability of profit assumes a lognormal distribution of the stock price at expiration based on the implied volatility you enter. All calculations run locally in your browser; nothing you type is sent to a server.
